Team Participation Coach

Paste a meeting transcript. See who talked, who didn't, and get coaching on how to even out participation.

📖 How to use this with your own team
  1. Record your meeting (Zoom, Teams, Otter, etc.) and copy the transcript.
  2. Make sure each line starts with the speaker's name and a colon, like Maya: I think we should.... Timestamps in front of the name are fine.
  3. Optional: paste your team charter or Breakout Learning feedback in the second box. The coach will check whether the meeting matched your norms.
  4. Click Analyze meeting. The bars come from your transcript's word counts; the coaching comes from AI.

Transcripts are sent to OpenAI to generate coaching and are not stored by this site. Remove sensitive details first.
